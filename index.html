Crimson Ascent

In the boundless crimson wilderness of Mars our isolation becomes both decree and revelation.

Blending hard science fiction with deep philosophical undertones, Crimson Ascent explores the unbreakable human spirit, the price of discovery, and the responsibilities of those who step beyond Earth.
